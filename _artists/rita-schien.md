Rita Schien, soprano, is a St. Louis-based vocalist and graduate of the University of Missouri- St. Louis, where she earned her Bachelor of Music in Vocal Performance. Her recent accomplishments include winning UMSL's Young Artist Concerto Competition in 2023, receiving a scholarship from Union Avenue Opera's CRESCENDO! Young Artist Program in 2024, and placing at the Central Region NATS Competition in 2024.

Rita is currently in her fourth season as a Soprano Young Artist with the Bach Society of Saint Louis, where she has performed in recitals, education concerts, masterclasses, and community events. Her recent solo appearances include Schubert's *Mass in G Major* at St. Francis de Sales and the title role in Warren Martin's *The True Story of Cinderella* with Ladue Chapel. Her operatic experience also includes performances with Winter Opera St. Louis in *The Magic Flute*, *The Sorcerer*, and *Un ballo in maschera*. She will return to Winter Opera St. Louis in 2027 for Puccini's *Turandot*.

Rita is excited to be part of Magpie Opera Collective and to continue developing her artistry while growing alongside fellow young artists.
