Soprano Olivia Knopf is a senior vocal performance major at Webster University. During her time at Webster, she has performed a number of roles, including Cendrillon in Viradot's _Cendrillon_, the First Witch and Dido cover in Purcell's _Dido and Aeneas_. Along with this, she recently was in the chorus in Floyd's _Susannah_ with Arch City Opera and the chorus in Gounod's _Romeo and Juliet_ with Winter Opera St. Louis. She will be making another appearance with Winter Opera in their upcoming show of Puccini's _Turandot_.
