Peter Sykes is a St. Louis-based pianist, singer, choral director, and educator. Originally from Los Angeles, Peter has lived in St. Louis since the summer of 2025. He holds a bachelor's degree in music and computer science from Yale University. While at Yale, Peter served as the music director of the Yale Whiffenpoofs, the oldest collegiate a cappella group in the United States. In addition to his work with Magpie, Peter is a collaborative pianist for the St. Louis Children's Choirs, a principal singer in the Bach Society of St. Louis, and a choir teacher at John Burroughs School. Peter is thrilled and grateful to make music with all the wonderful people of the Magpie Opera Collective.
