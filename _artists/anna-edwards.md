Anna Edwards is an accomplished singer, music director, and voice and piano teacher. Anna has a Bachelor of Music in vocal performance from Webster University. She is thrilled to continue singing with Magpie Opera Collective for her second season!

Anna has distinguished herself by winning several local and regional classical singing competitions, including CRESCENDO! in 2024, 2025, and 2026, as well as the Central Region National Association of Teachers of Singing (NATS) competition in 2025. Her most recent performance credit includes singing Cendrillon in Pauline Viardot’s *Cendrillon* with Webster University Opera Studio. She has also been serving as music director and curriculum developer for Wentzville Connection Theatre Company since 2021.
