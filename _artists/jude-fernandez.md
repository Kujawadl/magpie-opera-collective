Jude Fernandez is a senior at Webster University earning a Bachelor of Arts degree in Music, as well as a minor in Psychology. In their most recent Opera Studio performance at Webster, Jude sang the role of Armelinde in a production of Pauline Viardot’s _Cendrillon_. In the fall, Jude will be singing in the chorus of _Joshua’s Boots_ by Adolphus Hailstork with Opera Theater of St. Louis. Jude is an alumnus of the OTSL’s Artist in Training program for high school singers. In May, Jude returned to St. Louis from an 8-week long Study Abroad program in Vienna, Austria.
