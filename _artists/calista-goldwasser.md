Calista Goldwasser, mezzo-soprano, is one of the founders of Magpie Opera Collective. She holds a Master of Music from Webster University and a Bachelor of Music from DePauw University. For the past several years, Calista has held the title of Young Artist with the Bach Society of Saint Louis and sings in the opera choruses of Winter Opera of Saint Louis and Union Avenue Opera. Most recently, she performed as “Mrs. McLean” in *Susannah* with Arch City Opera Project and as “Zweite Knabe” in *Die Zauberflöte* with Winter Opera. In addition to performing, Calista also provides artistic and stage direction for Magpie Opera Collective, and she couldn’t be more grateful for your support of this artistic endeavor.
