Delanie Glover is an incoming senior at Webster University, pursuing her degree in Vocal Performance. This is her first Magpie production, and she is thrilled to get to work again with some of her closest friends and colleagues. Delanie was last seen on stage as Maguelonne in Webster University Opera Studio’s production of _Cendrillon_, and is now super excited to dive into Humperdinck’s _Hansel and Gretel_ as a member of the chorus and Gretel’s Act 3 cover!
