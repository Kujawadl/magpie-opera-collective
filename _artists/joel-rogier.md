Noted for his "fine voice and comic timing" and his "robust and confident demeanor," St. Louis-based baritone, Joel Rogier, is dedicated to sharing the joy and power of the human voice with audiences of all ages. While he is sad that he will not be able to attend this season's performances of *Bewitched* in person, Rogier is excited to make his first foray into directing in collaboration with all of Magpie Opera Collective's wonderful artists. This season Rogier can be seen with Winter Opera St. Louis in *The Most Happy Fella* (Pasquale) and *Turandot* (Mandarin) and as the Sea King in Opera for the Young's *Rusalka: A Mermaid's Tale*. Earlier this year, he appeared in the first preview performance of *Flame and Shadow: The Passion and Pain of Sara Teasdale* (Vachel Lindsay) at the St. Louis Art Museum. This summer, Rogier was featured at Union Avenue Opera in their house concert series, as the Rabbi in *Fiddler on the Roof* and as Second Philistine (and part of the "leading star" chorus) in *Samson et Dalila*. He also made his debut with Druid City Opera as the Notary and covering Dr. Malatesta in *Don Pasquale*. Favorite past roles at Union Avenue Opera include Harry Houdini (*Ragtime*), Crespel/Luther (*Les contes d'Hoffmann*), Mr. Lindquist (*A Little Night Music*), Moralès (*Carmen*), and First Soldier in *Salome*. With Winter Opera St. Louis Rogier has played Silvano (*Un ballo in maschera*), Paris (*Roméo et Juliette*), Boatswain Bill Bobstay (*H.M.S. Pinafore*), Speaker of the Temple (*Die Zauberflöte*), Harry Blake (*Naughty Marietta*), and Viscomte Cascada (*The Merry Widow*), alongside many other featured and ensemble appearances.

Rogier has dedicated a significant portion of his career to sharing opera with young audiences, appearing in many children's operas both at home with Opera Edwardsville and throughout the Upper Midwest with Opera for the Young, where he has appeared in shortened versions of such classic works as *The Elixir of Love* (Sgt. Bill Corey), *The Barber of Seville* (Figaro), *The Pirates of Penzance* (Pirate King and Major General), and *The Magic Flute* (Papageno). Other favorite past roles include King Melchior (*Amahl and the Night Visitors*), Count Almaviva (*Le nozze di Figaro*), Father (*Hansel and Gretel*), and the title role in *Don Giovanni*. Rogier has also performed with Southern Illinois Music Festival (SIFest), American Chamber Chorale, St. Louis Fringe Festival, Music On Site, and the Brevard Music Center.
