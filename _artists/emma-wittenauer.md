Emma Wittenauer is absolutely thrilled to work with the Magpie Opera Collective on this production! She is pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree in Music with an Emphasis in Voice with a minor in French at Webster University. She has most recently been seen as the Child in _Samson et Dalila_ with Union Avenue Opera, La Fée in Pauline Viardot’s _Cendrillon_ with the Webster University Opera Studio, and in the chorus of Carlisle Floyd’s _Susannah_ with Arch City Opera Project. Coming up, she will be playing the role of Miss Pearl in Adolphus Hailstork’s _Joshua’s Boots_ with the Opera Theatre of St. Louis. Emma is the 2026 Webster University Concerto and Aria Contest winner, as well as a Union Avenue Opera Crescendo! Young Artist Competition scholarship recipient in 2025 and 2026. Emma has received two second-place scholarships in Classical and Musical Theatre at the central region NATS competition and participated in her third residency with award-winning soprano, Christine Brewer. In May, Emma returned from studying abroad at Webster’s campus in Vienna, Austria!
