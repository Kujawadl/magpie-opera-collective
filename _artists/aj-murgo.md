A.J. Murgo is a tenor and conductor from Bristol, Rhode Island, currently based in St. Louis. A.J. received his Bachelor of Music in Vocal Performance from the Boston Conservatory and his Master of Arts in Music from Webster University, with a focus in Choral Conducting and Vocal Performance. Highlights of A.J.'s vocal engagements include performances with the St. Louis Symphony Chorus, the Choral Arts Alliance of Missouri's Prometheus ensemble, and Grammy-nominated vocal ensemble VOCES8. As a conductor, A.J.'s primary focus has been on choral music. Engagements include performances with the Webster University Singers, Emmanuel Episcopal Church, and Christ Church Cathedral, as well as the premiere of new music by composer Dragomir Page. In addition to his skills as a conductor, A.J. brings a wealth of opera experience to Magpie's production of _Hansel and Gretel_, drawing from his experiences performing the operas of composers such as Igor Stravinsky, Jake Heggie, Giuseppe Verdi, and Charles Gounod. Beyond his engagements with Magpie Opera Collective, A.J. is scheduled to appear throughout 2025 and 2026 in performance with the Christ Church Cathedral Schola, the Cathedral Basilica of St. Louis Schola Cantorum, and Early Music Missouri.
