Nancy Mayo is delighted to be part of Magpie Opera Collective’s second season – and back performing in the hall where she spent 20 eventful years as Adjunct Professor at Webster University. After receiving her MA in Piano Performance, she has been rehearsal pianist and chorus master for many companies: Columbia (SC) Lyric Opera, Hamburgische (Germany) Staatsoper, Union Avenue Opera, the Muny, Winter Opera St. Louis, Gateway Opera, and Opera Theatre St. Louis. She greatly enjoys helping companies present these wonderful works.

Currently, she maintains a private piano studio, is organist at Bonhomme Presbyterian Church, is involved with an opera premiere, _Flame & Shadow_ by Nika Leoni, and collaborates in recitals and recording projects.

Nancy is grateful to her mother, who gave up her own adult piano lessons so that Nancy could begin studying at age 5.
