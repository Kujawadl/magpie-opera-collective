Riley Cameron is an educator, conductor, and performer located in the St. Louis area. Born and raised in Kenosha, WI, he studied at Webster University under Dr. Jacob Lassetter. Riley is thrilled to return to this role after having been in the first act with Calista and Anna in 2024 in Webster's Opera Studio program. Since graduating from Webster, he has been teaching choir, piano, and general music at Metro Academic and Classical High School. Aside from his teaching duties, Riley has performed with Magpie in their inaugural showcase last fall, as well as with other local companies like Union Avenue Opera and Winter Opera St. Louis. Riley is also thrilled to be joining the Bach Society of St. Louis as a principal singer for this upcoming performance season.
