Audrey Sondag, a native of Norman, Oklahoma, graduated in the Class of 2026 from Webster University with a Bachelor of Music in Vocal Performance and a Minor in Dance. During her undergraduate studies, she completed an eight-week study abroad program in Vienna, participated in three residencies with Grammy Award–winning soprano Christine Brewer, and was the winner of Webster University’s 2025 Aria and Concerto Competition. Her professional credits include performances with Cimarron Opera Company, Winter Opera St. Louis, and Union Avenue Opera. As an emerging artist, Audrey is excited to gain exposure in performance spaces of all varieties and is a passionate advocate for accessibility, diversity, and representation in the arts.
