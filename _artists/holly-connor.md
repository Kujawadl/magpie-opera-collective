Holly Connor is a Soprano with roots in Seattle and St. Louis who studies with Dr. Jacob Lassetter. A senior at Webster University pursuing a Bachelor of Music in Vocal Performance, Holly has appeared with Webster University Opera Studio as La Fée in _Cendrillon_, Spirit in _Dido and Aeneas_, and chorus in _Susannah_ and _Suor Angelica_. She has also performed scenes as Zerlina in _Don Giovanni_, Servilia in _La Clemenza di Tito_, Adele in _Die Fledermaus_, and Susanna in _Le Nozze di Figaro_. Her greatest achievement to date was performing as a guest soloist with the St. Louis Symphony Orchestra.

Holly sings with the Bach Society of Saint Louis Chorus, Webster University Chamber Choir, and Webster University Jazz Singers. She also studies classical and jazz piano and is a student member of the National Association of Teachers of Singing (NATS). Her honors include First Place in the Webster University Orchestra Aria Competition, Semi-Finalist in the National Association of Teachers of Singing Classical Competition, and winner of an alumni scholarship from Opera Theatre of Saint Louis' Artists-in-Training Program. Holly has been a Young Artist in Union Avenue Opera's Crescendo Program for the past three years, and a Young Artist at Arch City Opera Project for the past year.
