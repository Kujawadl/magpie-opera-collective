Born in Hannibal, Missouri, Helen “Hedy” Rothfuss has been active in music since she began piano lessons at age 7. Hedy received her Bachelor’s degree in Music Education with an emphasis in piano performance from the University of Missouri - Columbia, and her Master of Arts in Music Education degree from the Catholic University of America with an emphasis in organ performance. Since her college years, Hedy has taught general music at the elementary and middle school levels for 25+ years and has been active as a solo performer, musical theatre and opera pianist, and piano accompanist in the Midwest and East Coast. Hedy has also served in music ministry positions in these regions for over 35 years. Hedy was just recently appointed as Director of Music at St. Martin Episcopal Church in Ellisville, MO, and is a collaborative pianist at St. Charles Community College (Cottleville, MO) and Missouri Baptist University (Creve Coeur, MO).
