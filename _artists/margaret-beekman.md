Margaret Beekman is so happy to be directing for Magpie Opera Collective’s second season. Her backstage credits include assistant stage manager for *Anna Bolena* with Winter Opera and Stage Manager for *Junie B Jones* at SIUE and *Seussical Jr.* at Tessitura Musical Productions. Some of her favorite on stage credits include Mabel in the *Pirates of Penzance* at SIUE, Professor Hidgens in *The Guy Who Didn’t Like Musicals* at SIUE, Rosalie Mullins in *School of Rock* at SIUE, and most recently Principal Soprano Ensemble in *Sweeney Todd* at Fly North Theatricals. She would like to thank her family and friends for their endless support.
