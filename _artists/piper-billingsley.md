Mezzo-soprano and Webster University student Piper Billingsley is a senior vocal performance major. She has performed a number of roles during her time at Webster, including Le Baron de Pictordu in Viardot's _Cendrillon_, the Second Witch in Purcell's _Dido and Aeneas_, and various other roles in opera scenes, including the Dritte Dame in Mozart's _Die Zauberflöte_. Billingsley's other experience includes various cover roles, including the Sorceress in _Dido and Aeneas_ and most recently the cover of Mrs. McLean in Floyd's _Susannah_ with the Arch City Opera Project. Billingsley will also be making her debut in the Winter Opera St. Louis' chorus in both Loesser's _The Most Happy Fella_ and Puccini's _Turandot_.

She hopes that everyone watching enjoys the show and the hard work that has been put into this production!
